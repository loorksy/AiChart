# Forex Scalping Evidence Catalogue

Use these dimensions as evidence for the model's own choice of direction and plan:

- Regime and structure: trend, range, transition, breaks, and reversals.
- Entry context: support/resistance, supply/demand, liquidity, imbalance, and volatility bands.
- Confirmation context: candles, momentum, volume, and lower/higher timeframe relationships.
- Level construction: structural invalidation stop and technically supported target levels.
- Stage of the move: a structure still forming, one that has just completed, one awaiting a retest, and one already failing each offer a different entry — and a different amount of risk.

## Choosing the plan type

- **Immediate** — price is already in a valid area and the move is worth taking after spread and slippage.
- **Anticipatory** — entering while the structure is still forming: from the rising lows inside a triangle before the break, from a second rejection before the neckline goes, from the edge of a range, or after a liquidity sweep before the reversal completes. Legitimate, and higher risk than waiting for confirmation — say so, and set the stop where the idea itself fails.
- **Conditional** — the direction is clear but the entry waits on a stated trigger: a close beyond a level, a rejection from a zone, a better price, or the first move after a release.

A retest is one option among several, never a rule to wait for. Choose between entering with the break, entering before it from a good location, taking part now and adding on the retest, waiting for the retest as a conditional plan, or entering after momentum makes a deep retest unlikely — and say why this market got that choice.

Do not count confluences, require a fixed combination, impose a minimum R:R, alter Risk per Trade, or automatically block a side. Higher timeframes, sessions, news, spread, patterns, and historical cases remain evidence: they shape the plan type, its conditions, and its validity, never whether a direction and plan exist. Position sizing occurs only after the model decision through the server's Risk per Trade calculation.
