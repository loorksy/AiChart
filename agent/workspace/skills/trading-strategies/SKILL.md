# Universal Trading Strategies Matrix (10,000 Configurations)

This skill provides a systematic framework for the Agent to construct, identify, and execute high-confluence trading setups by combining elements from four core technical dimensions. By selecting one option from each dimension, the Agent can instantiate any of the **10,000 unique strategy combinations**.

Every trade recommendation generated by the Agent MUST specify its configuration code (e.g., `Strategy Setup: [A3-B2-C1-D5]`).

### Dimension A: Market Structure & Trend Filters (High Timeframe Bias)
These options define the macro filter or trend direction. A trade must align with the HTF bias.

*   **A1: SMC HTF Order Flow / Market Structure (MS)**: Analyze structure using swing highs and swing lows on H4/D1. Establish bias based on Break of Structure (BOS) and Change of Character (CHoCH).
*   **A2: Multi-EMA/SMA Confluence**: Trend filter based on EMAs (20, 50, 100, 200). Bulish when price is above EMAs and EMAs are stacked sequentially; bearish when below.
*   **A3: Wyckoff Phase Identification**: Determine if the market is in Accumulation (Phase C Spring/Phase D SOS), Markup, Distribution (Phase C UTAD/Phase D SOW), or Markdown.
*   **A4: DXY & Beta Market Correlations**: Core trend correlation. Forex majors filtered against the US Dollar Index (DXY) strength; Altcoins filtered against Bitcoin (BTC) dominance and Beta coefficients.
*   **A5: Session-based Liquidity & Timeflow**: High Timeframe structure filtered by trading sessions (London Open, New York Open, Asian Range). Bias determined by sweeps of session highs/lows.
*   **A6: Volume Profile POC & Value Area (VA)**: Determine bias based on price relationship to High Volume Nodes (HVN) and Point of Control (POC). Bullish above POC; bearish below POC.
*   **A7: Liquidity Sweep Bias (HTF)**: Look for sweeps of major Daily/Weekly Highs or Lows. Bias is mean-reversion immediately after a clean sweep.
*   **A8: Bollinger Bands Expansion & Volatility Cycle**: Determine bias based on contraction (squeeze - preparing for breakout) or expansion (trending phase).
*   **A9: MACD Momentum Confluence (HTF)**: Macro trend defined by H4/H1 MACD line cross and histogram polarity (positive for long, negative for short).
*   **A10: ATR Volatility-Adjusted Trend**: Macro bias determined by Average True Range (ATR) bands or Supertrend indicators on H4 to filter noise.

### Dimension B: Key Entry Zones / Points of Interest (POI)
These represent the specific area on the chart where the trade execution is planned.

*   **B1: Order Block (OB) / Mitigation Block**: Unmitigated candle that initiated a strong move breaking structure. Look for refinement on lower timeframes.
*   **B2: Breaker Block (BB)**: A failed Order Block that was broken through by a strong impulsive move. Price returns to test the block as support/resistance flip.
*   **B3: Fair Value Gap (FVG) / Imbalance (BISI/SIBI)**: Inefficiency in price delivery. Entry zone targeted at the 50% equilibrium level (Consequent Encroachment) of the FVG.
*   **B4: Fibonacci Golden Pocket (61.8% - 78.6%)**: Entry zone located between the 61.8% and 78.6% retracement levels of the swing leg.
*   **B5: Support & Resistance Flips (S/R Flip)**: Classic horizontal price levels that previously acted as resistance, now tested as support (or vice-versa).
*   **B6: Pivot Points (Daily/Weekly S1-S3 / R1-R3)**: Key mathematical price levels based on prior session high, low, and close.
*   **B7: Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP) Bands**: Standard deviation bands of VWAP (typically 1st or 2nd deviation bands) serving as dynamic support/resistance.
*   **B8: High/Low Liquidity Pools (PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL)**: Previous Daily High/Low or Previous Weekly High/Low. Entry zone is right at the boundary or slightly beyond.
*   **B9: Volume Profile High Volume Node (HVN) / POC**: Entry placed exactly at major volume peaks where high liquidity and acceptance exist.
*   **B10: Dynamic Channels (Keltner Channels / Donchian Channels)**: Dynamic boundary lines serving as entry targets during overextended market phases.

### Dimension C: Execution Triggers & Confirmations (Low Timeframe)
These represent the short-term signal confirming that the price is reacting to the POI.

*   **C1: Lower Timeframe Market Structure Shift (LTF MSS)**: Wait for price to touch the POI on HTF, then look for a CHoCH/MSS on M1/M5/M15 with displacement.
*   **C2: Candlestick Pattern Confirmation**: Standard candlestick formations (Bullish/Bearish Engulfing, Hammer/Shooting Star, Pinbar, Morning/Evening Star) on the entry timeframe.
*   **C3: RSI / MACD Divergence**: Bullish or Bearish divergence between price action and RSI or MACD at the POI.
*   **C4: MACD Signal Line Cross / Histogram Acceleration**: Momentum cross on the entry timeframe confirming transition of power.
*   **C5: Order Book Depth & Delta Spikes**: Sudden cluster of buy/sell wall absorption or aggressive market order imbalance on footprint/delta charts (when available from broker feed).
*   **C6: Smart Money On-Chain Flow (Crypto)**: Aggressive whale deposits/withdrawals, exchange flow balance changes, or smart money wallet buy signals.
*   **C7: Funding Rate & Open Interest (OI) Divergence**: High funding rate crash or negative funding rate short-squeeze setups coupled with rising Open Interest.
*   **C8: Volume Breakout Confirmation**: Entry triggered when price breaks a local range with a volume bar at least 1.5x the 20-period average volume.
*   **C9: Stochastic RSI Oversold/Overbought Cross**: Low Timeframe Stochastic RSI crossing over in extreme zones (<20 for buy, >80 for sell) while at POI.
*   **C10: Moving Average Ribbon Alignment**: Confirmation when M5/M15 short-term EMA ribbons cross and fan out in the direction of the HTF bias.

### Dimension D: Risk, Money Management & Exit Profiles
These define how the position is sized, where the stop loss is placed, and how profits are taken.

*   **D1: Fixed Risk-to-Reward 1:2 (Conservative)**: Stop Loss set at invalidation point. Take Profit set exactly at 2.0x the risk distance. No adjustments.
*   **D2: Fixed Risk-to-Reward 1:3 (Standard SMC)**: Stop Loss set at invalidation point. Take Profit set exactly at 3.0x the risk distance.
*   **D3: ATR-Based Trailing Stop**: Stop Loss set at 1.5x - 2.0x ATR from entry. Trailing stop updates dynamically based on ATR volatility contraction/expansion.
*   **D4: Structure Invalidation Stop & Liquidity Target Exit**: Stop Loss placed below the structural swing low/high of the entry block. Take profit targeted at the opposing major liquidity pool (PDH/PDL or equal highs/lows).
*   **D5: Partial Profit (50% at 1:1 R:R) & Break-Even (BE) Adjust**: Take 50% profit at 1:1 Risk-to-Reward ratio. Move Stop Loss of the remaining 50% position to the entry price (Break-Even).
*   **D6: Session End Time Exit**: Trade is closed at the end of the London or New York trading session regardless of PnL to avoid overnight/weekend swap charges and spreads (primarily Forex).
*   **D7: News Pre-Release Exit**: Automatic exit of the position 10-15 minutes prior to high-impact economic news events (CPI, FOMC, NFP) to avoid slippage and wide spreads.
*   **D8: Pyramiding Scale-In**: Initial entry at 0.5% risk. Add 0.25% risk on every subsequent BOS on lower timeframe, trailing the aggregate stop loss to the new structural support.
*   **D9: Multi-Target Scaling Out**: Split position into three take-profit targets: TP1 at 1:1.5 R:R (30% size), TP2 at 1:3 R:R (50% size), TP3 at 1:5 R:R (20% size). Stop loss moved to break-even after TP1 hit.
*   **D10: Super-Conservative Risk Halving**: Reduce default trade risk (notional value) by 50% if trading during low-volume sessions (e.g. Asian session for non-JPY pairs) or when trading counter-trend.

## 📈 Confluence Combination Examples

The Agent should combine these parameters to suit the asset and market environment:

### Example 1: Crypto Futures Short Squeeze Setup (`A7-B4-C7-D5`)
*   **Concept**: Bullish reversal trading a liquidity sweep and negative funding rate.
*   **A7**: A Daily low is swept, creating a bullish mean-reversion bias.
*   **B4**: Retracement down to the 78.6% Fibonacci Golden Pocket on M15.
*   **C7**: Open Interest rises while Funding Rate goes deeply negative (indicating aggressive shorts trapped).
*   **D5**: Stop Loss below the swept low. Take 50% profit at 1:1 R:R, move stop loss to break-even.

### Example 2: Forex London Breakout Trend Continuation (`A5-B1-C1-D2`)
*   **Concept**: Classic SMC Order Block mitigation during London session.
*   **A5**: London session Open sweeps Asian Range low and establishes bullish structure.
*   **B1**: Refined H1 Order Block identified.
*   **C1**: Price taps the H1 OB, followed by a M5 CHoCH (Market Structure Shift) with strong volume.
*   **D2**: Entry on the M5 FVG inside the CHoCH. SL below M5 low. Take profit set at fixed 1:3 R:R.
